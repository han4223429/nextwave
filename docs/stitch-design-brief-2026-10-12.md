# NextWave Stitch design brief — 2026-10-12

The brief below contains only public club and project information. The generated screens are layout references; all facts, imagery, interaction and responsive behavior must be checked against the actual site before implementation.

## Direction comparison

- **A / Project archive:** A Korean editorial archive led by actual projects, restrained blue and orange, a strong text grid, generous white space, and a usable opportunity index. Select this direction because the club already has two real project demos and sourceable records.
- **B / Workshop noticeboard:** Layered flyers and pinned notes. Reject as it would obscure scanning opportunities and make small, repeated panels the dominant motif.

## Google Stitch draft

[Open the NextWave Editorial Club Portal draft](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/projects/11545932921380241285). Two screens were generated for a project-led homepage and a searchable competition portal. The submitted direction requested warm white and ink surfaces, cobalt blue, restrained orange, large Korean type, asymmetric grids, real MAYHEM and RESCUE JET project areas, and compact portal rows with source, title, deadline and original link. It explicitly discouraged fake achievements, stock photography, gradients, glass and repetitive rounded cards.

The first draft contained invented awards, metrics and product claims. A second instruction in Stitch asked it to remove invented metrics, patents, specifications, dates, coordinates and fake awards, and improve Korean type, whitespace, the project image area and opportunity list. The refined draft still contained generated imagery and copy. Neither was imported into the site. Only the editorial layout direction and palette informed `archive-v2.css` and `opportunity-index-v2.css`; actual content, portraits and project art are from the existing club site and verified source links.

The implementation keeps the public six-source opportunity board and member login behavior. All live counts, dates, source health and links still come from the existing feed and application code. This document is a design audit trail, not source evidence for club facts.
